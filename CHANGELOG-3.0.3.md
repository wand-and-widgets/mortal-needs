# Mortal Needs 3.0.3

A small update to give the new icons the space they deserve.

- Larger preset emblems and need illustrations, with a readable preview in smaller windows.
- Clearer dock controls, portrait feathers, and history icons.
- Larger need icons in broadcasts and the SessionFlow widget, with spacing adjusted for compact layouts.

Your characters, values, and rules stay as they are. Reload Foundry after updating to load the new styles.

Validation: 61 automated tests passed. Visual checks covered Foundry 14.368 with D&D 5e 6.0.5 at 1366×900 and 1024×768. SessionFlow checks used its widget renderer in an isolated layout fixture; full canvas integration was not retested for this styling patch.
